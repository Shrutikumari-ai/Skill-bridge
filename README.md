# 🌿 SKILLBRIDGE Ayurveda

### Integrated Skill Development, Education & Industry Collaboration Platform

SKILLBRIDGE Ayurveda is an integrated digital platform designed to connect
**Ayurveda students, academic institutes, and industry** through skill assessment,
personalized learning, internships, placements, skill-gap analysis, industry collaboration, 
and document verification.

The platform brings multiple workflows together in a single system to support the complete journey from
**skill assessment → learning → internship → placement → career development**.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

The platform provides dedicated portals for:

- 👨‍🎓 Students
- 🏫 Institutes / Academia
- 🏢 Industry
- 🔐 Document & Certificate Verification

It enables students to identify their skill gaps, access learning resources, 
apply for internships and jobs, and build verified career portfolios.

Institutes can monitor student progress, manage internships and placements, 
analyze skill demand, conduct training programs, and collaborate with industry.

Industry users can publish jobs and internships, manage recruitment, identify skill requirements,
conduct training programs, and collaborate with institutes and students.
